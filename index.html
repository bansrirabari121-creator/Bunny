A little about me

Creativity is my kind of magic ✨

Hey, I'm Bunny! ♡ I love the worlds that anime creates — the emotions, the tiny details, and the characters who stay in your heart long after the story ends. Through anime dubbing, I enjoy giving characters a voice and adding my own feeling to every line. Through drawing, I turn imagination into something you can see. I'm always learning, experimenting, and finding new ways to make something that feels uniquely mine. This little corner of the internet is where my voice, art, and ideas come together. Thanks for stopping by and being part of my journey. 🌷
